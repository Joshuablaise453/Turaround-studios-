About Us

Our journey, mission & values

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Turnaround Ministries started as a small home fellowship and has grown into a thriving community dedicated to seeing lives transformed by the Gospel. We believe in second chances and the power of God to rewrite stories.

Mission

To lead people into a life-changing relationship with Jesus, equipping them to impact their world.

Vision

To be a global beacon of hope, raising turnaround agents in every sphere of society.

Core Values

Love, Integrity, Excellence, Community, Prayer, Generosity.

Meet Our Pastors

Pastors Michael & Sarah Johnson

Lead Pastors with over 20 years of ministry experience. Their passion is seeing families restored and individuals ignited for purpose.

Pastor David Ochieng

Associate Pastor & Worship Director. He leads our worship team and young adults ministry with energy and depth.

Statement of Faith

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God. We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Salvation through Jesus Christ, His resurrection, and the empowering of the Holy Spirit for holy living.
